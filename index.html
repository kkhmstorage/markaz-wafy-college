About Our Institution

Pioneers of the Wafy Vision

KKHM Islamic & Arts College, situated in the serene Markaz Campus, Athavanadu, is a premier educational institution exclusively for boys. We hold a historic position as the very first college affiliated with the Coordination of Islamic Colleges (CIC), proudly bearing Affiliation Number 01.

As a center of religious-material synthesis, we offer the prestigious Wafy course. Our mission is to nurture students into responsible scholars and professionals by providing a well-rounded 8-year curriculum that blends advanced Islamic studies with recognized university degrees.

Principal's Message

The Wafy Curriculum

An 8-Year Comprehensive Program

Our unique curriculum for boys is divided into three major phases, seamlessly integrating Islamic theology with secular academic degrees.

1

Wafy Preparatory (First 2 Years)

Studied alongside Plus Two (Higher Secondary)

Computer Science

Combines foundational Wafy Islamic studies with Higher Secondary Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Computer Science.

Biology Science

Combines foundational Wafy Islamic studies with Higher Secondary Physics, Chemistry, Botany, and Zoology.

2

Wafy Degree (Next 4 Years)

Studied alongside University Degree Programs

B.Sc Physics

+ Wafy Degree

Explore the fundamental principles of the universe alongside advanced theology.

B.Sc Chemistry

+ Wafy Degree

In-depth study of chemical processes integrated with Islamic jurisprudence.

B.Sc Psychology

+ Wafy Degree

Understand human mind and behavior through scientific and moral perspectives.

B.A English

+ Wafy Degree

Dive into literature and language arts combined with Islamic philosophy.

B.A Sociology

+ Wafy Degree

Study of society and social institutions grounded in ethical frameworks.

3

Wafy PG (Final 2 Years)

Post Graduation in Islamic Studies

Post Graduation Specializations

The culmination of the 8-year Wafy course involves intensive 2-year postgraduate studies. Students delve deep into specialized departments such as Quran Interpretation, Hadith, Jurisprudence, Islamic History, and Arabic Literature, equipping them to become authoritative scholars and thinkers.

Eligibility & Procedure

  • For Wafy Preparatory (+2): Boys who have passed SSLC/10th standard or equivalent and clear the CIC Wafy Entrance Examination.
  • For Wafy Degree & PG: Continuation requires successful completion of the preceding Wafy phases and relevant secular examinations.
  • Procedure: Apply through the official CIC Admission portal, clear the entrance test, and complete the enrollment process at our campus.
