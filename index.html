About Our Institution

Pioneers of the Wafy Vision

KKHM Islamic & Arts College, situated in the serene Markaz Campus, Athavanadu, is a premier educational institution exclusively for boys. We hold a historic position as the very first college affiliated with the Coordination of Islamic Colleges (CIC), proudly bearing Affiliation Number 01.

The 8-Year Integrated Wafy Curriculum

An 8-Year Comprehensive Program

Our unique curriculum for boys is divided into three major phases, seamlessly integrating Islamic theology with secular academic degrees.

1

Stage 1: Wafy Preparatory (First 2 Years)

Studied alongside Plus Two (Higher Secondary)

Science Stream

Computer Science + Wafy Prep

Combines foundational Islamic studies with Higher Secondary Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Computer Science.

Science Stream

Biology Science + Wafy Prep

Combines foundational Islamic studies with Higher Secondary Physics, Chemistry, Botany, and Zoology.

2

Stage 2: Wafy Degree (Next 4 Years)

Studied alongside University Degree Programs

B.Sc Physics

+ Wafy Degree

Explore the fundamental principles of the universe alongside advanced theology.

B.Sc Chemistry

+ Wafy Degree

In-depth study of chemical processes integrated with Islamic jurisprudence.

B.Sc Psychology

+ Wafy Degree

Understand human mind and behavior through scientific and moral perspectives.

B.A English

+ Wafy Degree

Dive into literature and language arts combined with Islamic philosophy.

B.A Sociology

+ Wafy Degree

Study of society and social institutions grounded in ethical frameworks.

Language Proficiency

+ Translation Skills

Multilingual mastery in Arabic, English, Urdu, and Malayalam.

3

Stage 3: Wafy PG (Final 2 Years)

Post Graduation in Islamic Studies

Post Graduation Specialization

Specialized Disciplines of Islamic Scholarship

The culmination of the 8-year Wafy course involves intensive 2-year postgraduate studies. Students delve deep into specialized departments such as Quran Interpretation, Hadith, Jurisprudence, Islamic History, and Arabic Literature, equipping them to become authoritative scholars and thinkers.

Admission to the Wafy course is conducted through the centralized entrance exam of the Coordination of Islamic Colleges (CIC). Boys who have successfully passed SSLC / 10th standard can apply for Wafy Preparatory.

1

For Wafy Preparatory (+2):

Boys who have passed SSLC/10th standard or equivalent and clear the CIC Wafy Entrance Examination.

2

For Wafy Degree & PG:

Continuation requires successful completion of the preceding Wafy phases and relevant secular examinations.

3

Procedure:

Apply through the official CIC Admission portal (wafyonline.com), clear the entrance test, and complete allotment.
